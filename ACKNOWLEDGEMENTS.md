# Acknowledgements

Rhino.Inside-ArcGIS carries forward the idea demonstrated by
[ArcRhino](https://github.com/nicoazel/ArcRhino), an **AECTech 2019 Hackathon**
project that brought Rhino and Grasshopper into ArcGIS Pro.

Thank you to the original team: **Amit Nambiar, Eesha Khanna, Nico Azel, Ryan Welch,
and Sabrina Naumovski**. Their prototype demonstrated how Rhino's modeling and
computational tools could work alongside ArcGIS's spatial data tools.

See the [original project and demonstration](https://github.com/nicoazel/ArcRhino)
for the team's work and history. This repository is the current Rhino 8 / ArcGIS
Pro integration; ArcRhino remains the historical project.

Thanks also to Robert McNeel & Associates for
[Rhino.Inside](https://github.com/mcneel/rhino.inside), Rhino and Grasshopper, and
to Esri for ArcGIS Pro and its SDK. Rhino and ArcGIS Pro require their own licenses.
Third-party components retain their own licenses.
